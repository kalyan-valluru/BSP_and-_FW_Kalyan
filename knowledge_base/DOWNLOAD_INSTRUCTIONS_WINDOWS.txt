BSP Astra - Official demo documentation download

EASIEST METHOD
1. Extract the project ZIP.
2. Open the bsp_work folder.
3. Double-click DOWNLOAD_DEMO_DOCS.cmd.

COMMAND-PROMPT METHOD
Open Command Prompt inside bsp_work and run:

  node knowledge_base\download_all_demo_docs.mjs

Downloaded official vendor files are saved under:

  workspace\vendor_knowledge\raw\

Download status, errors and SHA-256 hashes are saved in:

  workspace\vendor_knowledge\download_status.json

POLICY
- Only official vendor domains are accepted.
- Redirects outside the official vendor domain are rejected.
- PDF documents are checked for a real %PDF signature.
- Login/error HTML pages are never saved as fake PDFs.
- Failed downloads remain PENDING_RUNTIME_DOWNLOAD and are not fabricated.
